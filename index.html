AOG events: the hardest documentation of all

Aircraft on ground. Clock running. Phone ringing. And you still have to document everything.

An AOG event is the worst time to write anything. You are troubleshooting a complex problem under maximum pressure while the company wants a live, timestamped report of every action, every phone call, every part ordered. Those two things should not have to happen at the same time.

With MX-Logbook you speak into your phone as events happen. "Called dispatch 0847, reported hydraulic fault left main gear, began troubleshooting per AMM 32-30-00." Voice to text, captured in real time. When the aircraft is back in service, generate a clean chronological AOG report in seconds and paste it directly into your company's shared document.
